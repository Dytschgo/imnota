# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Imnota to be a welcoming, useful project. Participants should be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualised conduct, personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
